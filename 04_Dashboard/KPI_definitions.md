# KPI Definitions

All KPIs are calculated from `superstore_cleaned.csv` (9,993 order lines). On the dashboard they respond to the sidebar filters.

| KPI | Definition | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| Total Sales | Revenue generated by all order lines | SUM(sales) |
| Total Profit | Profit generated by all order lines (negative when a line loses money) | SUM(profit) |
| Total Orders | Number of distinct orders | COUNT DISTINCT(order_id) |
| Total Customers | Number of distinct customers | COUNT DISTINCT(customer_id) |
| Quantity Sold | Total units sold | SUM(quantity) |
| Profit Margin | Share of sales kept as profit | Total Profit / Total Sales x 100 |
| Average Order Value (AOV) | Average revenue per order | Total Sales / Total Orders |
| Sales per Customer | Average sales per customer | Total Sales / Total Customers |
| Orders per Customer | Average number of orders per customer | Total Orders / Total Customers |
| Profit per Customer | Average profit per customer | Total Profit / Total Customers |

## Customer value segments
- **High-Value:** total sales at or above the 75th percentile and total profit above zero
- **Low-Value:** total sales at or below the 25th percentile
- **Medium-Value:** all other customers

## Cohort retention
- **Cohort:** customers grouped by the month of their first order
- **Retention % (month n):** customers in the cohort who ordered n months after their first order, divided by the cohort size

## Notes
- Orders and customers are counted as distinct IDs, never as rows.
- Products are analysed by product name, because some Product IDs are shared by several products.
